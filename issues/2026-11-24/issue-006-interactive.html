01Modular Code Gates Beat Prompt Rules and Full Rewrites at 2.5x Lower Cost

Markdown rules break down under multi-turn loop pressure. Google researchers built a unifying modular framework across four runtime files. Under Prompt-Only Reflection, agents ignored RULES.md and doubled unverified exits to 30.0%. Real Python gates on context, tools, and stop hooks fix that leak.

Modular Stage-3 gates also beat full rewrites on cost and transfer. Full rewrites raised turn caps to 28 and burned 451.6k tokens per task. Modular gated evolution kept the 12-turn cap and hit 40.0% at 46.7k tokens. Zero-shot transfer to Gemini 4 Argon reached 37.5% at 64.8k tokens.

02Co-Evolving Harness Code and Policy Weights Breaks Static Ceilings

Tuning only weights or only harnesses hits an early wall. Microsoft Research, UVA, and CMU introduced Self-HPO to alternate both steps. Each round runs an H-step harness edit before a P-step RL update. Hard tasks failed during RL seed the next harness search.

Joint evolution beat single-side tuning across three SWE benchmarks. On Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, Self-HPO hit 69.5% on SWE-Verified and 64.8% overall. Out-of-domain scores rose +5.5 points on SWE-Multi and +6.9 points on SWE-Pro. Meanwhile, isolated SFT distillation fell below base out of domain.

Verifier Co-Evolution · Training-Free

UCoE Co-Evolves Executable Verifiers Using Driving Capacity on Contrasts

Static verifiers go stale once an agent fixes its early mistakes. Researchers from Oregon State, Penn State, Johnson & Johnson, and AG2 AI built UCoE. It co-evolves an executable verifier program alongside a bounded harness experience block. Candidate verifiers must beat the current judge on paired response contrasts.

Selection uses driving capacity, the lowest margin across contrast families. On six open-ended suites, UCoE hit 59.00% win preference on Qwen3-8B. Verifiable tasks rose from 36.06% to 38.48% with fixed weights. Inside Codex on SpreadsheetBench-2, exact match rose from 17.3% to 22.7%.

Why It Matters
VEIO evolves Stage 06 verifier.py bundles across weekly cycles. Driving capacity ensures new checks beat old ones before replacing Seatbelt rules.

Open-World RSI · Contrastive Probes

Harness^2 Scales Two-Level Recursion Across Unseen Open-World Tasks

Real workflows rarely hand agents unit tests at start time. Google Cloud AI Research and Northwestern built Harness^2 for unseen tasks. Task-level recursion runs a contrastive Propose-Probe-Compose loop to compare harness edits. Harnessing-level recursion then distills winning edits into reusable cross-task memory.

Single-step edits lifted GLM-5 and Gemini 3.5 Flash across suites. Gains reached +11.5 points on WorkBuddy-Bench and +11.8 points on LAB. Parallel search (k=5) added +3.9 points for GLM-5 while trimming 2.7 steps. Gemini 3.5 Flash cut steps 13.8% on OpenCode and 15.5% on Codex.

Why It Matters
VEIO coordinates OpenCode, Codex, and Gemini builders across mixed tasks. Propose-Probe-Compose fills verifier gaps when repos lack tests.
